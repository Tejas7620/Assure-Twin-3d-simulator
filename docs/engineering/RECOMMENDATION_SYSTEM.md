# RECOMMENDATION_SYSTEM.md
## The Certified Recommendation Contract & Explainability Architecture

In petroleum operations, field engineers will not implement recommendations generated by a "black box". If software tells a superintendent to change a pump speed, the superintendent will immediately ask:
- *"Why this specific speed?"*
- *"Why didn't you pick a faster speed to get more oil?"*
- *"What assumptions are you making about downhole conditions?"*
- *"Who takes responsibility if the rod parts?"*

ASSURE-TWIN answers all four questions through the **Certified Recommendation Contract** and the **Why / Why-Not Explainability Engine**.

---

### 1. The 10-Section Recommendation Contract Structure
Every valid recommendation generated by ASSURE-TWIN is formatted as a formal, self-contained engineering contract:

1. **Header & Case Metadata:** Unique Case ID (`REC-2026-BGW-004`), Well Identifier (`BGW-17A`), Asset, Target Formation (`Jodhpur Sandstone`), and Timestamp.
2. **Current Baseline State:** Current SPM ($3.6$), Stroke ($74''$), Oil Rate ($17.6\text{ BOPD}$), Water Cut ($12.8\%$), Near-Wellbore Temp ($72.8^\circ\text{ C}$), In-Situ Viscosity ($1,392\text{ cP}$).
3. **Proposed Operating Controls:** Recommended SPM ($2.8\text{ SPM}$), Recommended Stroke ($74''$), Steam Cycle Timing ($t_\text{steam} = \text{Day } 22$).
4. **Expected Forward Outcomes (30D):** Projected Oil Rate ($20.8 \pm 1.8\text{ BOPD}$), Net Gain ($+2.2\text{ BOPD}$), SOR Improvement ($-17.2\%$), Float Margin ($12.5\%$).
5. **Causal Engineering Rationale (Why Recommended):** Step-by-step physical reasons why this setpoint is beneficial.
6. **Counterfactual Rejections (Why Not):** Explicit mechanical reasons why competing alternative settings were rejected.
7. **Operating Preconditions:** Required wellhead pressure bounds and electrical grid stability before setpoint dispatch.
8. **12-Point Gatekeeper Verification:** Full checklist confirming all 12 safety bounds passed.
9. **Model Domain & Provenance Attestation:** OOD score ($0.23$), Physics/ML consensus delta ($4.5\%$), Data provenance tags.
10. **Cryptographic Lineage Seal:** SHA-256 digital signature and Senior Production Engineer sign-off block.

### 2. The Explainability Engine: *Why* & *Why Not*

#### A. Causal Physics Explanation (*Why Recommended*)
Generated by `backend/app/assurance/why_engine.py`:
- *"1. Maintains safe buoyant rod float margin of 12.5% (above 10.0% safety floor) during the downstroke in 1,392 cP heavy oil."*
- *"2. Derating speed from 3.6 to 2.8 SPM reduces annular Couette shear drag across the 1,000 m rod string by 22%."*
- *"3. Sustaining long 74-inch stroke preserves volumetric pump displacement while reducing cyclic reversal stress on the rod taper."*
- *"4. 30-day forward rehearsal confirms that well trajectory remains entirely within the Preferred green operating envelope."*

#### B. Counterfactual Rejections (*Why Not Alternative Settings*)
- **Alternative A (8.5 SPM - Aggressive Production):**  
  *Rejected:* Downstroke rod float margin collapses to $3.2\%$ (severe compressive buckling and rod-parting hazard).
- **Alternative B (1.5 SPM - Ultra-Conservative):**  
  *Rejected:* Inflow velocity insufficient; downhole pump fillage drops to $42\%$, causing severe fluid pound shock.
- **Alternative C (Immediate Steam Injection):**  
  *Rejected:* Reservoir enthalpy reserve remains at $23.5\%$; re-steaming today wastes ₹12 Lakhs in natural gas fuel and increases SOR to $5.4\text{ m}^3/\text{m}^3$.
