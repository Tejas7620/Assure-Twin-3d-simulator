# PPT_PROJECT_TRACEABILITY.md — Master Traceability Matrix: PPT vs. Actual Project

This master matrix audits every technical claim in the SIH presentation against the actual ASSURE-TWIN software repository.

### Allowed Status Values
- **MATCH:** The claim is fully implemented, verified, and functioning in the codebase.
- **PARTIAL:** The claim exists in concept or partial implementation, but is missing core elements claimed in the slide.
- **MISSING:** The claim is featured prominently in the PPT but has zero implementation in the codebase.
- **EXTRA:** The feature is implemented and functional in the codebase but omitted entirely from the PPT.
- **CONTRADICTORY:** The PPT claims a specific technology or architecture, but the codebase implements a different one.
- **UNVERIFIED:** The claim cites specific operational metrics or field numbers without empirical validation data.
- **OUTDATED:** The PPT reflects an obsolete prototype concept superseded by the current architecture.
